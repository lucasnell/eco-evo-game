Dispersal Lab: Aphids & Parasitoids

You're running a computational experiment on two cages of pea aphids (Acyrthosiphon pisum) and their parasitoid wasp (Aphidius ervi), based on Nell et al. 2024, Science. Each cage starts with two aphid clones: susceptible (grows faster) and resistant (survives parasitism more often, but grows a little slower). You don't choose which clone wins — you only control how much aphids and wasps move between the two cages. Watch what that choice does.

Setup

Split of the initial aphid population in each cage, before anything runs.

Controls (δₐ, γ)

Fraction of winged aphids that move between cages each day.

How unevenly wasps spread themselves across cages, and how tightly they track local aphid density.

You can adjust these between turns — they're the only levers you ever get. Clone frequencies change only from what actually happens to the aphids.

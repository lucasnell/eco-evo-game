Farm Manager: The Yield Trap

You manage a farm growing a crop that pea aphids damage. A natural enemy — the parasitoid wasp Aphidius ervi — already lives on your land and attacks the aphids for you. Your goal: maximize total crop yield. Your one lever is how much you invest in habitat connectivity between fields (hedgerows, field margins, corridors) so the wasps can move around and find aphids.
